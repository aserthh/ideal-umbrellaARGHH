Questions.

How do I activate my key?

After receiving your key, contact our bot via DM and type !activate (followed by your key), you will then be granted access to the client download.

What happens when my plan ends?

The launcher tells you the key has expired and stops loading the client. Buy a new key whenever you’re ready to continue. Lifetime keys never expire.

Can I use my key on another PC?

A key binds to the first PC it signs in on. If you change your hardware, contact support and your HWID can be reset.

Which versions are supported?

Currently, Ozark supports two versions: Forge 1.8.9 and Lunar 1.8.9, although a version that supports newer versions of Minecraft is in development.
